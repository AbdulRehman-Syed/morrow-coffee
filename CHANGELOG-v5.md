# Morrow v5 — The Slow House

## Why this revision
The previous site emphasized long paragraphs and continuously moving 3D objects. This version creates a clearer coffee-house first impression: photography, coffee, buying and brewing.

## Main changes
1. Cinematic **three-slide image hero**, manually controlled instead of auto-rotating; green, copper and parchment art direction.
2. Ten optimized **locally stored WebP images** showing coffee, brewing, product bags and a coffee-bar concept.
3. Functional **five-destination navigation**: Coffee, Our Café, Brew Lab, Origins, Journal. Back/Forward works; mobile navigation is full screen and clear.
4. **Shoppable collection**: roast filters, search, sort, product pages, tasting notes, bag quantities and subtotal.
5. **Café content**: coffee-shop gallery and switchable espresso, filter and pastry sample menu. No fabricated address or opening hours.
6. **Origins**: original interactive atlas plus a restrained animated flavor radar.
7. **Brew Lab**: existing step-by-step Three.js experience retained, code-split from the homepage, with gentler motion and lighting and lower WebGL resolution.
8. **Journal**: readable brewing articles in accessible modal dialogs.
9. **Search**: full-screen coffee search and keyboard Escape support.
10. **Performance**: no perpetual hero canvas, reduced-motion support, static local photos, no forced smooth-scroll wrapper.

## Notes
All storefront/catalog data, café menu prices and visuals are sample content. Cart works locally; newsletter and checkout are explicit demonstrations, not connected transactions. The v4 source is preserved at `reference/App-v4.tsx`. Webflow/Adobe integration files remain.
